Chemistry for Children and Teenagers

There are many different school classes, ranging from basic required courses to elective "passion projects." Practical science and chemical experiments provide students with unique insights into how the physical world operates.

At Millennia World School, we believe that early exposure to interactive science fosters critical thinking, problem-solving skills, and a lifelong passion for discovery. During our hands-on chemistry sessions, students engage in safe, age-appropriate laboratory practices guided by experienced mentors.

"Science is not just a subject in a textbook; it is a mindset of questioning, experimenting, and understanding the world around us."

Key Highlights of the Workshop

Students had the opportunity to explore safe chemical reactions, learn about molecular structures through visual models, and practice safety protocols in a modern lab setup:

  • Interactive Safety Protocols: Understanding chemical labels and protective equipment.
  • Acid & Base Indicators: Experimenting with natural plant-based indicators.
  • Molecular Model Building: Hands-on creation of basic water and carbon molecules.

The event concluded with a student showcase where young participants presented their mini-experiments to parents and peers, demonstrating incredible enthusiasm and confidence.

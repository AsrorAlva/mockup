Admissions

Welcome to the beginning of your child’s educational journey at Millennia World School. We accept applications throughout the academic year.

How to Apply

We make our enrollment process as transparent and simple as possible to help families make informed decisions about their children's futures.

  • 1. Schedule a Tour & Inquiry

    Visit our Ciputat campus, observe classes in action, and meet our academic leaders. You can book via the form on the right.

  • 2. Registration & Assessment

    Submit registration documents and arrange an assessment date. For early years, this is a soft observation session. For older kids, a fundamental review is administered.

  • 3. Interview & Decision

    A friendly chat with parents to align values, followed by admission decision and placement confirmation within 5 business days.

  • 4. Enrollment & Welcome

    Complete fee payments, obtain school uniforms, and attend our student orientation day.

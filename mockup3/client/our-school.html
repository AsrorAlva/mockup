Our School

We believe that a child’s world should grow larger every day. Millennia World School provides a supportive, inquiry-led bilingual ecosystem.

Founded with the vision of bridging global perspectives and rich cultural values, Millennia World School offers a progressive education path from preschool to junior high school. Our sunlit Ciputat campus is custom-built to serve as an interactive learning laboratory where lessons naturally flow from class studios to gardens and open courtyards.

By combining international benchmarks with local heritage, we prepare students to navigate a complex, changing world with confidence, empathy, and intellectual independence.

Quick Facts

  • Student-Teacher Ratio

    1:10 average to ensure personalized care.

  • Dual Pathways

    Cambridge Curricula and National Assessments.

  • Bilingual Instruction

    English and Bahasa Indonesia active language environment.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

"To be a progressive, compassionate educational community that inspires children to discover their unique potential, embrace global mindedness, and act with integrity."

Our Mission

  • Nurture Inquiry: Foster self-directed curiosity and critical thinking through collaborative projects.

  • Build Character: Embed values of compassion, respect, and global citizenship in our daily interactions.

  • Provide Caring Guidance: Design safe, inclusive spaces where kids feel confident growing at their own pace.

MWS Core Values

Curiosity

We support questioning, active testing, and searching for answers in and out of the studio.

Compassion

As a Charter for Compassion partner, we practice daily kindness, active listening, and service to others.

Collaboration

Learning is shared. We build team projects that teach conflict resolution and mutual growth.

Mindfulness

Developing self-awareness, emotional regulation, and deep focus in academic and social pursuits.

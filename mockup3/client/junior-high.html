Junior High

Fostering academic rigor, analytical laboratory research, and student-led community initiatives to build future readiness.

In our Junior High school (Grades 7 to 9), students take direct charge of their learning. We transition children from structured guides into independent researchers. The Cambridge Secondary Curriculum establishes rigorous benchmarks in mathematics, analytical sciences, and written English, preparing kids for the transition to high school networks.

Students design and execute complex research projects. They tackle real-world themes, such as urban sustainability, coding automation, and global trade economics. These projects are presented publicly at student exhibitions, fostering critical public speaking and bilingual communication skills.

Key Program Features

  • Academic Independence & Rigor

    Developing specialized subject research, data analysis, scientific logging, and formal essay writing styles.

  • Student Councils & Leadership

    Active participation in student organizations, organizing campus green events, debates, and charity projects.

  • High School Alignment

    Personalized academic profiling and consulting to help parents and kids transition smoothly to leading senior high schools.

STEAM Exhibition 2026

Student Life Published on October 16, 2026 • Written by MWS Editorial Team

This term, our Elementary and Junior High school students collaborated on a cross-disciplinary project: designing real-world solutions for waste reduction and energy efficiency.

The annual MWS STEAM Exhibition has grown from a showcase of science fair boards into an interactive event where children demonstrate working mockups. Drawing inspiration from our ecological school values and our collaborations with international climate networks, students spend six weeks researching, building, and testing their projects.

"We didn't just want to build volcano models. We wanted to look at our school campus cafeteria waste and build a composting filter system that we can actually use." — Kianna A., Grade 7 Student Coordinator

Class studios were transformed into interactive laboratories. Visitors were guided through prototypes of solar-powered study desks, automated garden sprinklers, and community compost bins. Junior High students walked parents and visitors through the engineering concepts behind their setups, utilizing their bilingual presentation skills to explain variables in both English and Indonesian.

Grade 6 students explaining composting and chemical filtration properties.

By putting students in the role of planners, creators, and public speakers, the STEAM Exhibition builds collaborative skills and teaches kids how to resolve creative friction. This aligns with our core belief that education should equip students with practical competencies and character values to make a tangible difference in their local communities.

We want to extend our deepest gratitude to the teachers who facilitated the studios, the parents who offered constant support, and the students who proved that curiosity, when backed by care and guidance, yields outstanding results.

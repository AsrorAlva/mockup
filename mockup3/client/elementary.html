Elementary

Bridging solid academic disciplines with inquiry-led projects, research, and global peer collaborations.

In our Elementary school, students build a deep foundation in core academic competencies while developing critical research habits. The curriculum blends international benchmarks with local requirements. Math, Science, and English utilize Cambridge frameworks, while Civics, Indonesian, and social studies align with the National Curriculum guidelines.

We organize learning into 'Units of Inquiry' (UOI). For instance, rather than studying weather systems in isolation, kids examine how local agricultural practices adapt to changing monsoons, combining geography, data collection, and biology in a single collaborative project.

Key Program Features

  • Unit of Inquiry (UOI) Abstractions

    Cross-disciplinary project units where kids explore global topics, compile findings in logs, and present models publicly.

  • Active Laboratory Science

    Hands-on experiments in campus garden plots and laboratory benches, fostering natural observation over memorization.

  • Responsive Classroom Guidance

    Establishing daily class charter agreements, conflict resolution training, and parent-teacher collaboration plans.

Daily Schedule

Elementary schedules are structured to offer solid math block learning in the mornings, followed by project labs and outdoor play.

Program Overview

Our Elementary program prepares children to transition into analytical middle school years. We focus on building learning confidence and project management.

  • Age Group: 6 - 12 Years (Grades 1 - 6)
  • Class Size: Max 20 students
  • Schedule: 07:30 AM - 01:45 PM
  • Bilingual Focus: 70% English / 30% Indonesian

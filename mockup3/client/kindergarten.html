Kindergarten

Nurturing natural wonder, playful discovery, and emotional confidence in our youngest learners.

In our Early Years program, we view children as natural-born scientists and storytellers. Our curriculum focuses heavily on hands-on discovery and sensory play. Kindergarten classrooms are designed as open study studios, with direct access to sunlit patios and small garden beds where kids can plant seeds and interact with nature.

We integrate the 'Responsive Classroom' framework to help kids develop vital social-emotional skills, such as active listening, sharing, and expressing emotions constructively.

Key Program Features

  • Emergent Literacy & Bilingualism

    Storytelling, shared reading, and vocabulary enrichment in both English and Indonesian, building a natural foundation for bilingual reading.

  • Sensory Mathematics

    Exploring numbers, weights, and spatial dimensions through building blocks, water play, and physical sorting activities.

  • Social-Emotional Workshops

    Collaborative play and circle assemblies that teach negotiation, empathy, and active listening skills.

Daily Schedule

Our schedule balances structured group activities with open choice playtime, ensuring children build self-directed play skills.

08:30 – 09:30 Discovery Studios Sensory play, block building, art, and motor skills practice.
09:30 – 10:00 Snack Time & Social Care Independence, table manners, and cleaning up.
10:00 – 11:00 Outdoor Exploration & Gardening Physical growth, science observation, and team play.
11:00 – 11:30 Story Circle & Dismissal Literacy, reflection, and quiet mindfulness before home.

Program Overview

Our Kindergarten program establishes a warm foundation for children. We focus on natural curiosity and security to make their transition away from home smooth.

  • Age Group: 2 - 6 Years
  • Class Size: Max 15 students
  • Schedule: 08:00 AM - 11:30 AM
  • Bilingual Focus: 50% English / 50% Indonesian

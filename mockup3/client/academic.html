Academics

Learning at MWS is not passive. It is an active, inquiry-led experience where every child is treated as a capable and curious thinker.

Our academic ecosystem integrates the Cambridge International Curriculum with the Indonesian National framework, delivering both internationally recognised standards and a deep appreciation for local identity and values. Rather than teaching subjects in isolation, we design learning experiences that cross disciplines and connect academic rigour with real-world relevance.

We assess progress through evidence of learning, collaborative projects, self-reflection, and measured milestones — not simply through tests. Our goal is to build confident, independent learners who carry their skills far beyond the classroom.

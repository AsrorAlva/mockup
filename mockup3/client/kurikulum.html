Our Curriculum

We offer a customized, integrated curriculum structure combining global rigor, local identity, and deep character development.

At Millennia World School, we believe that children should grow into capable, compassionate global citizens. To achieve this, our curriculum does not simply stack worksheets. We systematically integrate the Cambridge International Curriculum, the Indonesian National requirements, and specialized social-emotional character pathways.

This integration ensures our graduates achieve excellent cognitive and linguistic capabilities, remain deeply grounded in their local heritage, and act with integrity and empathy toward their communities.

The MWS Tri-Pillar Curriculum

  • 1. Cambridge Framework

    English, Mathematics, and Sciences taught using inquiry-led, analytical secondary standards.

  • 2. National Core

    Indonesian Language, Civics, Social Studies, and Religion aligning with the Ministry of Education.

  • 3. Character & Compassion

    Responsive Classroom, Charter for Compassion principles, and Climate Action project modules.

Curriculum Component Details

Cambridge Pathway

Designed to develop kids who are confident, responsible, reflective, innovative, and engaged. We follow Cambridge frameworks to ensure international standard diagnostics in:

  • English Language Acquisition
  • Primary and Secondary Mathematics
  • Applied & Laboratory Sciences

National Alignment

We celebrate national identity and cultural heritage. Our students sit for national examinations and gain rich civic understanding via:

  • Bahasa Indonesia (formal and literary)
  • Pendidikan Pancasila & Kewarganegaraan (PPKn)
  • IPS & Local Environmental Geography
  • Pendidikan Agama (Values-aligned religious classes)

Compassion & Eco-Action

Learning is directed toward active positive contribution. Character education is built right into the daily academic flow:

  • Charter for Compassion: Collaborative empathy exercises
  • Climate Action Project: Global classroom eco-projects
  • Responsive Classroom: Positive social structures

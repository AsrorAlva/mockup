CURRICULUM & FRAMEWORK

Learning as an Adventure, Not a Chore

Millennia World School uses a Finnish Waldorf-inspired framework integrated with the Indonesian National Curriculum. Subjects are connected across disciplines so children can build context, meaning, and deeper understanding.

The curriculum combines age-appropriate academic rigor with rich artistic experiences, allowing creative young minds to grow through inquiry, expression, and purposeful practice.

Framework Focus

Differentiated learning, creative academics, artistic experience, and real-world relevance.

How Learning Is Designed

Our pedagogical model recognizes that children have different talents, dispositions, and forms of intelligence. Learning is designed with depth, relevancy, and connection to the world they live in.

01

Integrated Subjects

Concepts are not taught in isolation. Literacy, numeracy, sciences, arts, and social understanding are woven together to create richer learning experiences.

02

Differentiated Learning

From the introduction of alphabets to algebra, teaching is adapted so students can learn with depth, confidence, and personal relevance.

03

Creative Exploration

Academic learning is supported by artistic experiences that develop imagination, expression, focus, and emotional growth.

04

21st Century Readiness

Students are guided to love learning while developing critical thinking, collaboration, communication, creativity, and resilience.

Project Based Learning

PBL engages students in real-world problems and challenges so they can acquire deeper learning, stronger academic skills, and the confidence to create meaningful work.

Authentic Questions

Students investigate complex questions and issues that connect classroom learning with real situations, communities, and personal responsibility.

Voice, Choice & Reflection

Projects include student agency, sustained inquiry, reflection, critique, revision, and opportunities to present a final product.

The Four Cs

Learning experiences intentionally build critical thinking, communication, collaboration, and creativity as daily habits.

PRINCIPLES OF ASSESSMENT

Assessment Designed to Improve Learning

Assessment at Millennia is an ongoing process throughout teaching and learning. It is intended to help students understand their strengths, recognize areas for growth, and develop a lasting love of learning.

Assessment should be deliberately designed to improve and educate student performance, not merely to audit.

LEARNING PROCESS

Formative Assessment

Formative assessment focuses on the learning process, not simply right or wrong answers. It helps children become aware of how they learn and what they can improve.

  • Teacher observation and learning conversations
  • Student reflection on strengths and growth areas
  • Feedback during project and classroom activities
ACHIEVEMENT EVIDENCE

Summative Assessment

Summative assessment is used to understand achievement, but it works alongside formative feedback so progress remains the center of the learning journey.

  • Project outcomes and final products
  • Progress documentation and portfolio review
  • Student, parent, and teacher progress conversations

Sustainable Life Skills

Sustainable living helps children understand their local environment, their place in the wider world, and how their actions can create a positive impact.

Students learn about environmental footprints, responsible use of materials and resources, and the Sustainable Development Goals. They are encouraged to see themselves as part of a global community where even small contributions can help make the world better.

Practical experiences such as growing food, understanding the science behind farming, and cooking what they grow help develop responsibility, curiosity, and useful life skills.
